I read books so you don't have to — but that's not quite right.

What I actually do is read books so you *want* to. Every week, I take the best non-fiction books I can find and turn them into something you can absorb in 12 minutes: the core argument, why it matters, and what changes when you actually apply it.

This isn't about skimming. It's about catching the ideas worth carrying with you — the ones that shift how you think about decisions, habits, relationships, or the systems you live inside.

Beyond the blog, I build things with AI. I create open-source datasets and fine-tune language models — work you'll find on my [Hugging Face profile](https://huggingface.co/WILL-NAPOLINI-HF). If you're into ML, NLP, or just curious about what happens when books meet fine-tuning, there's something there for you.

**What you'll find here:**

📖 **Book Summaries** — Structured breakdowns of non-fiction books: key arguments, takeaways, and quotes that stick.

🧠 **AI Datasets & Models** — Open-source datasets and fine-tuned models on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/WILL-NAPOLINI-HF), built to bridge the gap between reading and doing.

📂 **Thematic Collections** — Deep dives into topics like decision-making, resilience, habit formation, and more.
